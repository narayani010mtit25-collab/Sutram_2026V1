SUTRAM 2026

Problem Statement

Building next-generation solutions where artificial intelligence meets digital investigation and forensic science.

NATIONAL HACKATHON THEME & BACKGROUND

Theme: AI-Powered Digital Investigation and Cyber Forensics Technologies for India

India is witnessing unprecedented growth in cyber-enabled crimes, including financial frauds, social media crimes, ransomware incidents, digital impersonation, deepfake-enabled offences, online radicalization, and crimes involving encrypted communication platforms. Investigating agencies and forensic laboratories are increasingly challenged by the volume, diversity, and complexity of digital evidence generated from mobile devices, cloud services, social media platforms, multimedia content, and emerging technologies.

The proposed National Hackathon aims to develop indigenous, AI-enabled cyber forensic solutions that assist Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs), Cyber Crime Investigation Units, Forensic Science Laboratories (FSLs), and judicial stakeholders in acquisition, analysis, correlation, reconstruction, and reporting of digital evidence while maintaining forensic integrity.

Final Round Demonstration Requirements

All teams reaching the final round will be required to demonstrate comprehensive technical rigor:

  • Working Prototype Functional execution of core AI & forensic modules
  • Source Code Clean, well-documented, reproducible repository
  • Documentation Forensic methodologies, setup manuals, and architecture
  • Investigation Dataset Benchmark sample data illustrating parsing and analysis
  • Cybercrime Scenario Demo Live demonstration using a realistic cybercrime scenario
  • LEA Deployment Roadmap Practical rollout strategy for police and FSL stakeholders
